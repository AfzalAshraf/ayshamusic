# Favorite downloads

On Android, newly favorited songs are queued and persisted independently of Telegram. One background worker selects the highest-bitrate direct compatible AAC/M4A stream the existing approved resolver offers, and writes those bytes without re-encoding. This is not a guarantee of the source's original master or of all available formats; unsupported, restricted, challenged, over-64-MiB or unavailable tracks fail visibly. No proxy, identity rotation or bot-check bypass is used. Extra data (including mobile data) and up to one full staging copy plus a public copy are required.

A successfully published file is in `Music/Aysha Music` and remains after app uninstall. Android 10+ uses MediaStore pending/publication; Android 8–9 requires the legacy storage permission. The public filename is based on the video ID to avoid overwriting another song; MediaStore also receives title and artist. Unlike cancels pending work where possible but never deletes an already published song; an in-flight publication may finish. Duplicates are avoided without overwriting existing files. The favorite/download queue is persistent across process restarts. Existing pre-upgrade favorites were session-only and cannot be recovered after that session ended; there is no silent bulk download.

Settings shows queued, downloading, saved and failed items, approximate byte progress and an explicit Retry button. Work runs while the app process is alive; it is not guaranteed to continue after Android stops the process. Download failure, permission denial, source refusal and insufficient space do not stop playback or delete Telegram messages. Temporary incomplete files are removed; public completed files are not automatically removed.

Offline Rust and emulator fixture tests do not prove live provider downloads, actual Android 8/9 permission behavior, public-file visibility on every phone or full-song playback. Do not request or rotate access to a challenged video without authorization. An installed APK update also requires matching signing keys.
